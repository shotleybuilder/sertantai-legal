## **Compliance Architecture Layer Model**

| Layer  | Layer Name                               | Purpose                                                      | Key Entities                                       | Core Properties                                      | Key Parameters / Fields                                                                    | Inputs                                              | Outputs                                  | Ownership                    | Notes                                                                   |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **L1** | **Obligations (Legal Source + Derived)** | Establish authoritative set of applicable legal requirements | Laws, Regulations, Clauses, Obligations            | Legal source integrity, traceability, status         | Law ID, clause ref, jurisdiction, status (in force/repealed), obligation text, source link | External legal data (ENHESA / provider)             | Structured obligation dataset            | Compliance / EHS Governance  | Must separate **source law vs derived obligation**; key integrity layer |
| **L2** | **Risk & Prioritisation**                | Classify and prioritise obligations based on risk signals    | Risk profiles, obligation tiers, signals           | Risk scoring, tiering logic, aggregation             | Risk score, enforcement activity flag, PR signal, criticality tier, confidence level       | External intelligence (risk feeds), L1 obligations  | Prioritised obligation set               | Risk / Compliance Leadership | Enables **risk-tiered proportionality**                                 |
| **L3** | **Controls**                             | Define how obligations are implemented operationally         | Controls, procedures, policies                     | Control type, ownership, linkage to obligations      | Control ID, type (preventive/detective), owner, frequency, linked obligation(s)            | L1 obligations, internal processes                  | Control framework mapped to obligations  | Ops / Functional Owners      | Critical bridge between compliance and operations                       |
| **L4** | **Evidence**                             | Capture proof that controls are executed                     | Evidence records, documents, data                  | Source, timestamp, completeness, automation          | Evidence ID, control ID, source system, timestamp, evidence type                           | L3 controls, internal systems (docs, sensors, logs) | Evidence repository (linked to controls) | Ops / Assurance              | Should aim for **evidence-by-design**                                   |
| **L5** | **Assurance**                            | Verify that controls are effective                           | Audits, reviews, test results                      | Coverage, independence, effectiveness rating         | Assurance ID, control ID, outcome, findings, reviewer, frequency                           | L3 controls, L4 evidence                            | Assurance outcomes & findings            | Internal Audit / Assurance   | Must link back to obligations via controls                              |
| **L6** | **Events & Change Intelligence**         | Detect and respond to triggers (internal & external)         | Regulatory updates, incidents, enforcement actions | Event type, impact, severity, linkage to obligations | Event ID, type, affected obligations, severity, date, trigger source                       | External intelligence, internal incidents           | Triggered actions, risk updates          | Risk / Compliance Function   | Core of **event-driven compliance**                                     |
| **L7** | **Decisions & Governance**               | Record decisions, accountability, and oversight              | Decisions, approvals, risk acceptances             | Traceability, accountability, rationale              | Decision ID, obligation/control link, rationale, approver, risk acceptance                 | Inputs from all layers (esp. L2–L6)                 | Governance record, escalation outputs    | Leadership / Governance      | Enables **decision traceability & ethical framing**                     |
