# run-01 step 4: the unread tail, ranked (2026-10-27)

These are the sentences and list leads the reader withholds entirely (class `unread:*`), by coarse shape (`items.tsv`, kinds `sentence` and `lead`).

- 920 occurrences fall into 757 shapes. 714 of those shapes appear in one law only, 30 in two, 8 in three or four, and 5 in five or more.
- **It is a true long tail.** Almost every withheld sentence is its own wording, so writing reader rules shape by shape would pay off little. Withholding keeps them safe: an unread statement holds back its provisions' bound and never sets a wrong one.
