  - title: "counterparty position means the governed entity is affected, not obligated"
    detail: >
      When fractalaw classifies an actor as counterparty rather than active, it means
      the provision creates an obligation ON someone else (e.g. a government authority)
      that AFFECTS the governed entity. The governed_only filter correctly excludes these
      because the customer's people don't have a duty to act — they're the beneficiary.
      Don't confuse governed_actors (flat legacy field) with actors struct position.
    tag: sync
  - title: "governed_actors flat field and actors struct can disagree on who bears the duty"
    detail: >
      The flat governed_actors field on legal_articles contains all non-Gvt actors mentioned
      in the provision. The actors struct adds position (active/counterparty) which determines
      WHO has the obligation. A provision can have governed_actors = [Org: Owner] but the
      Owner is a counterparty, not the active duty bearer. The sync filter uses the struct,
      not the flat field — this is correct.
