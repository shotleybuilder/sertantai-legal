# Session: Issue #163 Application Fields (CLOSED)

## Problem

The screener needs **application** (where a law operates), not extent. Examples:
- The Smoke-free (Premises and Enforcement) Regs extend to E+W but apply to England only.
- A WSI extends to E+W but applies in Wales.

Fractalaw will derive application and publish three new taxa fields (ZENOH-SPEC v2.4). Legal must have the columns and the subscriber ready first, or the publish fails.
