# Session: Pipeline to v0.1 — Meta (ACTIVE)

## Problem

v0.1 pushes **every law, whatever its age**, to production. The release split is by fix: bugs and unknowns affecting laws from 2002 on (the changes-feed era) are fixed for v0.1; bugs known to affect only older laws are held to v0.2 (Jason 2026-10-21). The AR measure dry run (`backend/data/reports/lat-refactor/ar_measure_final*`) sized what is left. This meta orders the road from here to v0.1 sign-off. It takes over the sequence of LR-00's "plan to data quality done" (A8 → D2).

## Rules

- **Cut line:** a defect goes in the v0.1 path when it affects laws from 2002 on (UK, or EU retained law); held to v0.2 when it is known to affect only older laws. Feature work (applying changes ahead of legislation.gov.uk) is v0.2 whatever the year.
- **Version tag:** each session in the LAT data pipeline carries `release: v0.1 | v0.2` in its frontmatter.
- **Corpus runs:** iterate on the affected laws; preview only the laws a fix can change and splice into the last full report; **one** relabel for v0.1 (memory: no needless corpus runs).
- Dry run → Jason → restore point → apply. Notes are pointers, never facts.
