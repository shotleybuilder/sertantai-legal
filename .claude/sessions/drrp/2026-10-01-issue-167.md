# Session: Issue #167, LAT change contract (CLOSED)

Spec: fractalaw `docs/architecture/DRRP-TEMPORAL-PROPOSAL.md` section L (7658933). D1–D4 approved (Jason, 2026-10-01). fractalaw tracks its side in fractalatai#73 (`parsing/2026-09-30-issue-73.md`).

## Problem

fractalaw's LAT sync sees only text diffs, so it can't tell repealed from live provisions (it infers repeal from dots), why a row changed (amendment, parser change, scope, correction), or when a change took legal effect. Without that, `as_amended` ("my obligations now") can't be trusted, and version history would fill with parser churn.
