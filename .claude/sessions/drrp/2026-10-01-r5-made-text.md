## Agreed design (fractalaw, 2026-10-01)

- `legal_articles_made` table, separate; current LAT never touched. Same structural columns and section_id convention as LAT. Made-only provisions keep the ids they had when made. Per-law made hash. No change fields.
- **Selection: live amended laws only.** lat_count > 0, has effects, and LRT `live` not revoked. No revoked laws, under Jason's standing rule (no earlier text for dead laws; their verdict stands).
- `mix lat.made`: `--law A,B`, `--sample N`, `--all`; caches the XML; replaces rows per law.
- **Sample:** ~20 live amended hub laws, weighted to heavy amendment, plus a few lightly amended ones for spread. **Excludes the 20 gold-benchmark laws.**
- Zenoh: `lat-made/{law}` (same schema as `lat/{law}`, Arrow/JSON); `lat-made-manifest/{law}` and `/*` (row_count, made_hash, fetched_at).
