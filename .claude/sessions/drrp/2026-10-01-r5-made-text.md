# Session: Made-text exception path (PENDING)

Was "R5: made text for amended laws" (bulk, ~800 laws). **Jason dropped the bulk fetch on 2026-10-01.**

## Jason's position (2026-10-01)

- **`is_making` is the law's character.** It separates laws that impose substantive requirements from laws that only commence, amend or revoke. Repeal or revocation never removes it, and amendment rarely does. The knot came from conflating repealed/revoked with "no longer making".
- **No scraping of old versions in bulk.** The existing law-level DRRP fields mean "everything the law imposed, as far as our text shows" (including later-repealed provisions). `current_*` covers live provisions only.
- **A smell:** a live, amended law that comes back `no_obligations` or `empowering`. Amending and commencing laws rarely get amended themselves: another amending law amends the amendments. Such a law is a candidate for fetching its made text.
- **Manual approval, per law.** Fetching has been a structured UI process and is moving to AI-through-API. Made-text fetches stay manually approved by Jason.
- **New laws:** history accrues from enactment through the per-row LAT change log (#167).
- **Separate feature, not now:** apply unapplied amendments from the amending law's own text (#171).

## Feasibility (kept for when a case is approved)

- `/{type}/{year}/{number}/made/data.xml` parses with `LatParser.parse/2` unchanged.
- MHSWR 1999: 76% of made rows identical to current. HSWA 1974: 38% identical, 471 made-only rows.
- 4–8 s per law to fetch and parse both versions.
