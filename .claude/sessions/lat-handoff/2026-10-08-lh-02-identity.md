## Problem

QA run 1 found `section_id`s (fractalaw's join key) wrong in three ways: Acts of Senedd Cymru (`asc`), `mwa` and `ukla` parse as SIs (`reg.119(1)`, "[Revoked]"); a child with a narrower RestrictExtent makes parallel detection qualify a whole provision with no alternative version (`reg.3(2)(c)[E+W]`); a Welsh Version whose inner P1 says RestrictExtent="E" is labelled England (uksi 2010/2214 reg.36, colliding ids). Fix before hand-off — ids changed later break enrichment keys.
