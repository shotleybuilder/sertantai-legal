# Session: Issue #166 Scoped LAT Parsing (PENDING)

## Problem

Large Acts are often relevant only in part (e.g. the Companies Act 2006 for environmental reporting: `part/15/chapter/5` is 92KB against a 13.5MB whole body). Legal keeps no record of a law's parse scope, so every LAT parse fetches the whole body, and a law parsed partially by hand would silently widen on the next re-parse. (UK_ukpga_2003_21, the Communications Act, is a whole-body parse: Parts 1–6, 10,900 rows, no schedules.)
