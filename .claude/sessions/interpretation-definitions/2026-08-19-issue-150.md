# Session: Issue #150: Bare 'the Act' References in SIs (CLOSED)

## Problem

70 definitions in Statutory Instruments reference "the Act" without naming it — e.g. "given by section N of the Act". The CitationExtractor returns nil because there's no law title to extract. In SIs, "the Act" almost always means the parent Act identified by the `enacted_by` field on the SI's legal_register record.
