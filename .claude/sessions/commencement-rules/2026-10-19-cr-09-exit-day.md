# Session: CR-09: Exit Day Read as IP Completion Day (CLOSED)

## Problem

Subordinate legislation made before exit day that comes into force "on exit day" is dated 2020-01-31 (11 p.m.); EU(WA)A 2020 Sch. 5 para. 1(1) reads such a provision as IP completion day (2020-12-31, 11 p.m.) unless the instrument expressly disapplies it (para. 1(2)) or regulations vary it (para. 1(3)). 34 of CR-06's remaining note-date disagreements (SI 2018/1392, 2019/837, 2019/512, 2018/1251, SSI 2019/26 …); LR-33 lists 9 laws as named-day starts.

Decided (Jason 2026-10-19): "Use 'on exit day' = 31 December 2020 … run as separate session".
