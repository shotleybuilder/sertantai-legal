## Cross-repo decisions (2026-09-25)

- **Extent is not application.** Legal owns `geo_extent` (the fix is #162). Fractalaw owns *application*: where a law operates, e.g. an E+W SI that applies to England only. Fractalaw will publish it as its own field, via a ZENOH-SPEC change. QQ-04's jurisdiction gate uses application, not extent.
- **Amending SIs that insert duties into a principal instrument are not Making** (QQ-01 policy).
- **Generic material codes are fitness-parser bugs** (Jason, 2026-09-25). The fix is in fractalaw's extraction, not compliance's evaluator. This is now the main screener lever: 94 QQ tree-condition misses, 67 with drafting vocabulary such as `regulations`, `person`, `offence` and `application`.
- **Historical fractalaw `duty_type` is stale** (pre-hub regex aggregates), so the QQ-01a backfill treats it as legacy evidence only.
