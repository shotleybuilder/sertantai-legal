## Jason's decisions (2026-10-11)

1. **"Revoked in part will not mean full revocation."** Implemented in `EffectTerritory` as: an "in part" effect on the *whole instrument* (no provisions named) is never `:effect_everywhere` evidence.
   - Narrowed from the literal reading because of the Salmon Act (1986/62): "repealed in part" effects there name "s. 1-3", "s. 5-30"… (asp 2003/15, S). legislation.gov.uk's "in part" is about the Act; the provisions it names are wholly repealed, and their 317 rows stay excluded.
   - Corpus: 5 laws had in-part `:effect_everywhere` claims. Only 2012/3038 changes: 486 rows excluded E+W+S+NI → nil (live), and reg.46(1)(a)(iv) ×2 → E+S+NI / W+S+NI (matching their notes).
2. **1965/57 Part ids** `pt.1`, `pt.1n1`…: OK for now.
3. **Wave 4 approved.**
