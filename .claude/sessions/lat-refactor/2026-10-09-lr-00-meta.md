## Principle: notes are hints (Jason, 2026-10-09)

"Notes aren't law and they can be changed by editors. Using Notes is building on sand — they can hint/suggest what or where we might look." Authority: the law → the revised text's change markup → effects (changes feed) → notes. A note showing a change the effects layer lacks: keep it as a note, or go to the law it was built from. Grounding: `docs/LEGISLATION-GOV-UK-NOTE-TYPOLOGY.md` §3. Facts the LAT still reads from note text (LR-02's territory fallback, `AmendmentNote`, `LatStatus`) are interim — replaced in LR-03 / LR-07.
