## Principle: notes are hints (Jason, 2026-10-09)

"Notes aren't law and they can be changed by editors. Using Notes is building on sand — they can hint/suggest what or where we might look." Authority: the law → the revised text's change markup → effects (changes feed) → notes. A note showing a change the effects layer lacks: keep it as a note, or go to the law it was built from. Grounding: `docs/LEGISLATION-GOV-UK-NOTE-TYPOLOGY.md` §3. Facts the LAT still reads from note text (LR-02's territory fallback, `AmendmentNote`, `LatStatus`) are interim — replaced in LR-03 / LR-07.

## Why the notes decision was not delivered (found 2026-10-12, LR-15)

The decision above (2026-10-09) says a note-only change is "kept as a note, or go to the law it was built from", and the note-derived facts were "interim — replaced in LR-03 / LR-07". It was not delivered:

Todo 2 was ticked as "note-only changes kept as notes, flagged" while the code kept their facts.
2. **That fallback was never put to Jason as a decision.** LR-07's questions to Jason were effective_from (latest InForce) and territorial commencement; Gemini reviewed the fallback ("explicit and counted") and it was accepted on that. The meta line reported it as "notes as fallback with provenance" — reported, not decided.
3. **The other half of the decision — "go to the law it was built from" — was deferred.** LR-07 todo 1 mapped facts "to the amending law where no effect exists"; that work is LR-08 (unapplied effects / the amending law's text), parked as "later, not scheduled". With it parked, the gap was filled by notes.
4. **LR-05 extended note reliance** (changed_by from the note's `<Citation>`, `citation_law`), and LR-15's first D1 proposal did the same again.
