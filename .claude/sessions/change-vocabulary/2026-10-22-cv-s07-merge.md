## State at start (2026-10-28)

- **Merge** (`ChangeVocabulary.Merge`, 328 lines). It joins in three ways:
  1. by effect id and target;
  2. an id-less change (the law's text, a legacy markup ChangeId) joins the one change on its provision, above or below it, with the same operation family by the same law, or stays apart;
  3. same-day events by one law are told apart by the amending provision the feed names.
- **OH&S conflict census** (`lat.vocabulary --conflicts`, 149 laws): 45 `:unjoined` (49 in CV-04) and 661 `:unread`. The unread are feed changes the law text doesn't read: coverage (CV-07), not the merge's.

  - KJ2: six law-text changes with an identical feed effect by the same law, left apart.

## What good looks like (proposed 2026-10-28, for Jason)

Aim: one change per real event, whichever sources state it. The merged change is the unit. Its faults:
- **over-join:** one change holds two events' claims;
- **split:** one event in two changes;
- **misplaced:** one event placed on different provisions by its sources.

Strata, computed (as `CheckStrata`), by a new `mix lat.merge_check --family F --strata`:
- **J, joined:** changes stated by two or more sources. Over-join risk.
- **U, unjoined:** the census `:unjoined` items. Split risk.
- **C, candidates:** single-source changes beside another change with the same law, same amending law, the same provision or one above or below it, and the same operation family. Split risk the census doesn't flag.
- The rest (single-source, nothing beside it) involve no merge decision and are not checked.

Pass, decided before the first round (CV-S06 model):
- U and C in full: no wrong merge left unfixed or unclassed. Known classes: the feed lists an effect twice (K6); distinct events.
- J by agent sample, pooled since the last fix: under 2% wrong changes and under 5% wrong (law, amending law) pairs at the 95% Clopper–Pearson upper bound.
- Golden from inputs 27/27.
- Published: a quality statement and the join rate (how many law-text and markup changes found their feed effect).

A `merge-check` skill holds the agent prompt, known classes and seed history.

### Round 1 verdict: FAIL

The faults are splits by four merge causes, plus reader faults surfaced on the way:
- **M1:** URI id alias. 13.5% of stored feed effects carry a URI id different from their EffectId, and the markup uses the URI id.
- **M2:** a markup effect id held by no feed record (183 OH&S markup-only changes).
- **M3:** the id-less join counts copies as several candidates:
  - markup repeated per version, and legacy ids sharing a CommentaryRef;
  - one effect staged into two rows;
  - a heading effect beside its body effect.

  It also never narrows by the amending provision.
- **M4:** an operation-less feed "amended" effect has no family.
- **M5:** two over-joins where the id-less join ignored the amending provision.

Reader faults:
- markup: an Addition whose commentary says "repealed";
- markup: "2004/ 1822";
- law text: a two-operation clause (CV-S06's open bug).

Root: identity. The effect id is the join, and the fallback asks only "the one change near here". The amending provision (the law-text key, the feed's AffectingProvisions, the markup commentary's citation) is in every source, and it is the event's identity beside the id.

## Identity design (round 2 fix; Jason: "Design doc + review first", reader faults fixed in this round)

The design is `docs/CHANGE-IDENTITY-DESIGN.md`. An event's identity is its effect id (`EffectId` or `URI`), else its source provision (amending law, amending provision), with copies collapsed into one candidate before any partner is chosen. New pure modules `Merge.Candidates` and `Merge.Partner`; `Merge` becomes the orchestrator.
