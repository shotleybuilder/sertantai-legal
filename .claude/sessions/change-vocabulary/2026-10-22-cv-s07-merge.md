## What good looks like (proposed 2026-10-28, for Jason)

Aim: one change per real event, whichever sources state it. The merged change is the unit. Its faults:
- **over-join:** one change holds two events' claims;
- **split:** one event in two changes;
- **misplaced:** one event placed on different provisions by its sources.

Strata, computed (as `CheckStrata`), by a new `mix lat.merge_check --family F --strata`:
- **J, joined:** changes stated by two or more sources. Over-join risk.
- **U, unjoined:** the census `:unjoined` items. Split risk.
- **C, candidates:** single-source changes beside another change with the same law, same amending law, the same provision or one above or below it, and the same operation family. Split risk the census doesn't flag.
- The rest (single-source, nothing beside it) involve no merge decision and are not checked.

Pass, decided before the first round (CV-S06 model):
- U and C in full: no wrong merge left unfixed or unclassed. Known classes: the feed lists an effect twice (K6); distinct events.
- J by agent sample, pooled since the last fix: under 2% wrong changes and under 5% wrong (law, amending law) pairs at the 95% Clopper–Pearson upper bound.
- Golden from inputs 27/27.
- Published: a quality statement and the join rate (how many law-text and markup changes found their feed effect).

A `merge-check` skill holds the agent prompt, known classes and seed history.
