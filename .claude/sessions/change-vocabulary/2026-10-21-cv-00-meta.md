# Session: Change Vocabulary — Meta (ACTIVE)

## Problem

Jason 2026-10-21: "Abstracting all change sources into a common vocabulary that we can just 'read' is the only way to do the labelling reliably." Today a change to a provision is known from five sources: CLML markup (Repeal/Addition/Substitution, Extent, RetainText, ChangeId), the changes feed's effects, the amending law's text, commencement (own clause, orders, feed), and notes (pointers only). They are merged twice, by different code: the parse (`EffectIndex`, `EffectTerritory`'s claim order, rule 3, orphan joins) gives each row's text, `excluded_extent` and `markup_status`; the register (`ChangeCollector` → `lat_change_log` → `LabelProjection`) gives the dates and periods, with today's territory taken from the parse (LR-26 D1′). The two merges hold pairwise reconciling rules (rule 3's gate, the claim's step order, J2 "markup wins today", J5) and can disagree. V1-04's territory audit found two such cases: Coronavirus Act 2020 s.78 and SI 2016/1105 reg.72(2)(b). Each fix so far has added another rule at an edge ("we've again slipped into chasing edges in code rather than abstracting").

The commencement ruleset already has the shape: several readers (`OwnClause`, `Order`, `Commencement.Effects`) write one `Commencement.Rule`, and one evaluator dates them. The amendment ruleset has one reader: `Amendment.Reader` reads the amending law's text. AR-00's design review was right for its scope, which was replacing the text matcher for amending instructions; the feed and the markup were never in it. `Amendment.Rule` has no territory: it is added afterwards, one way for register records (`Collect.territory`) and another in the parse.

## Goal

One vocabulary for a change, written by a reader per source and merged by identity (the effect id joins markup, feed and amending law). Each attribute (operation, words or whole provision, targets, territory, date, in force, savings) carries its evidence, and one stated evidence order per attribute replaces the pairwise rules. The parse's territory, the register and the labels all read the merged changes. The parse keeps only what only it can do: placing changed words in the text.

**Simplify (Jason 2026-10-21, on CV-01's inventory):** "lots of refactoring to keep things simple. Stopping all this duplication that's happened." Each attribute is decided once, by one reader per source and one evidence order. The duplicated vocabularies go: six operation verb lists, three qualification parsers, three ways of choosing a date, three range expansions, two type-to-territory maps. So do the stand-in rules for the missing identity (rule 3's gate, AR-04's compensators, J2, T1's blanking).

**Source authority (Jason 2026-10-21):** "The actual legal text is primary. These other sources are derived and secondary."
- **Tier 1, primary:** the legal text. The amending law's instruction, its extent and application clauses, its commencement provisions and orders, and the amended law's own extent and application clauses (C6/C11).
- **Tier 2, derived:** legislation.gov.uk's revised-text markup.
- **Tier 3, derived:** the changes feed.
- **Notes** are pointers only.

Decided:
- **The primary text wins** for every attribute our readers read from it. Derived sources fill only where the primary text isn't held or wasn't read. Every primary–derived disagreement goes to a conflict report, never silently reconciled ("which is probably how leg.gov.uk build their change feed and we will likely have most labels set that way").
- **Every label value carries its tier** (primary / derived), so consumers see which facts rest on legislation.gov.uk's editorial work.

## Rules

- Design before code: CV-01's architecture review and plan, reviewed by Gemini, signed off by Jason, before any child starts.
- Correct metadata from the data that best provides it; no rule exists only to make two sources agree.
- TDD for the extraction (red against the new modules, green without changing assertions).
- Dry run → Jason → restore point → apply; one re-parse and one relabel for v0.1.

## The workflow and the pass rule (2026-10-22)

Each step has an aim, and "what good looks like" is defined at the start of its session: a hand-checked sample from the OH&S core family, plus measures each defined in words with a test. The step is built until its check passes reliably; the session doesn't close before that, and the next step doesn't start until it does. Long runs (rebuilds, re-parses, fetches, scoreboards) are asked for first. Nothing goes to production before steps 1–11 pass and the shadow comparison (CV-05/06) is judged.

### Recipe for a new family (each step's check, in order)

1. **CV-S01 Sources:** `mix lat.sources --family F` (`--fetch` only with Jason's go). Pass: every need held, or absent upstream.
2. **CV-S02 Feed reader:** the `feed-reader-check` skill.
   - First the pattern catalogue (CV-S02b): `mix lat.feed_patterns` after the family's feeds are stored. Its new patterns and changed readings are decided (accepted, fixed, or pending with the correct outcome) until `FeedPatternsTest` passes.
   - Then the gate, placement (our faults 0), repeatability by a new seed, golden where held, and the agent on 50 for facts against the law text.
   - Pass: no WRONG outside the known patterns.
3. **CV-S03 Markup reader:** the `markup-reader-check` skill.
   - First the catalogue (`mix lat.markup_patterns`); new patterns are decided until `MarkupPatternsTest` passes.
   - Then the gate and placement (`mix lat.markup_check`, our faults 0), repeatability by a new seed, golden, and the agent on 50.
   - Pass: no WRONG outside the known patterns.
4. **CV-S04 Commencement reader:**
   - First both catalogues: `mix lat.commencement_patterns` (own commencement sentences, about 12 min) and `mix lat.order_patterns` (commencement orders, about 30 s). New patterns are decided (accepted, or `pending: <why>`) until `CommencementPatternsTest` and `OrderPatternsTest` pass.
   - Then the readers' re-read (ask first): own clauses and orders, then the rebuild.
   - Then `mix lat.commencement_check --family F` (rows: our faults 0; gaps: every one with a reason), golden, and the agent on 50 + 50 for a new seed.
   - **Pass (the stopping rule, Jason 2026-10-24):** no NEW defect in the agent sample; every remaining gap in a counted pending class. A family's new forms extend the catalogues.
5. **CV-S05 Extent and application reader:** the `extent-reader-check` skill.
   - Catalogue (`mix lat.extent_patterns`), extents, golden, `mix lat.extent_check` (our faults 0; sweep: no read-form miss), the whole-store census (CV-S05e when built), then agent seeds of 50 + 50.
   - **Pass (the statistical rule, Jason 2026-10-28):** pool the seeds since the last fix batch, every NEW fixed; the 95% upper bound of wrong-bound changes < 2% and of laws < 5%. Record the coverage table and a quality statement (CV-S05's is the model).
6. **CV-S06 Amending-law reader:** the `amending-reader-check` skill.
   - Catalogue (`mix lat.amendment_patterns --family F --accept`; every reading change reviewed), pre-flight for a fix (`mix lat.amending --read --preview --regression`, a rules diff of the laws touched), read pass, rebuild and **golden from inputs** (`mix lat.vocabulary --golden`, never only `--check`), census (`Conflicts`).
   - Strata (`mix lat.amending_check --family F --strata`): A agreeing, D disagreeing, L law text only, with the agreement rates.
   - D and L checked in full (agent batches of 50, `--stratum D|L`); A by agent samples of 50 (`--stratum A`, a new seed each).
   - **Pass (revised check, Jason 2026-10-28):** no misread left unfixed in D or L; A pooled (items unchanged since checked) under 2% wrong changes and 5% wrong pairs at the 95% upper bound; golden from inputs bar logged limits. Record the strata, agreement and a quality statement (CV-S06's).
7. **CV-S07 Merge:** the `merge-check` skill.
   - `mix lat.merge_check --family F --strata` gives the strata J (joined: an over-join risk), U (unjoined) and C (candidates: split risks), the join rate, and the spread tell-tale (keys under more than 3 top provisions).
   - U and C are checked by agent in full. J is sampled in batches of 50 on a fresh seed.
   - **Pass (Jason 2026-10-28):** U and C with no wrong merge unfixed or unclassed; J pooled since the last fix under 2% wrong changes and 5% wrong pairs (95% upper, so at least 149 with none wrong); golden from inputs 27/27.
   - Items unchanged since checked carry over. Record a quality statement (CV-S07's).
8. **CV-S08 Evidence:** the `evidence-check` skill.
   - `mix lat.evidence_check --family F --strata` gives the strata V (value conflicts, the law against legislation.gov.uk), D (markup against feed with no primary claim, by pattern) and A (agreeing, several sources), and the winning source per attribute.
   - V is checked by agent in full; D by pattern (`--per 10`); A in samples of 150 (5 × 30) on a fresh seed.
   - **Pass (Jason 2026-10-29):**
     - V and D: no wrong winner, false conflict or wrong pattern left unfixed or unclassed;
     - A: pooled under 2% wrong changes and 5% wrong pairs (95% upper);
     - golden from inputs 27/27.
   - **Gap rule:** a value no table source claims is a gap, published by cause, not WRONG.
   - Items whose chosen values are unchanged since checked carry over (a before/after snapshot of the sample).
   - Record a quality statement (CV-S08's).
9. **Later steps** (CV-S09 …): each step's session defines its check and adds it here when it passes on OH&S.

For every step:
- ask before long runs;
- record results in the family's step session;
- raise new parser tell-tales as measured patterns and fix them in a sub-session straight after;
- take only NEW defects to Jason, one at a time.
