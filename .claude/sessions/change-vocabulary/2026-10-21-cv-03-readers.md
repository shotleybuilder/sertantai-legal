## Source authority (Jason 2026-10-21)

"The actual legal text is primary. These other sources are derived and secondary." The order is: the law's text, then legislation.gov.uk's markup, then the changes feed. Notes stay pointers.
- The primary text wins.
- Every disagreement goes to a conflict report.
- Every label value carries its tier.
- The amending-law reader becomes the main reader. In the CV-02 table it was last.

Scoreboard so far: 4 → 8 (commencement, prospective, removals in part) → 12 (words repealed version the text; whole-law targets; insertion credit; undated start) / 27, all on derived sources.

**The amending law's text, primary (2026-10-21):**
- `Readers.AmendingLaw` reads the stored `Amendment.Rule`s.
  - A words change lands on its deepest named target; a provision change on its top-most.
  - Each change is dated by the amending law's own commencement text, with the feed's coming-into-force records excluded as derived.
- It reads exact targets: the 2008 Act's insertion names (4A)–(4D), never (4BA). That is V1-05's range bug, fixed at source by reading the law.
- **This flips an earlier decision.** `Commencement.Amending` was "effects first" (Jason 2026-10-18: "if we have the 'effects' data … why pull the laws too?"). Inside the vocabulary the law's own clause and orders now come first and the feed fills gaps, following the 2026-10-21 authority decision.

## C6 built (2026-10-22, Jason: "Build c6")

- **`ApplicationClause` gained two forms;** `parse/1`, which live status uses, is unchanged.
  - `parse_dated/1`: "From 1st January 2021 these Regulations apply in Northern Ireland only, in relation to …". The date is read, and a trailing subject-matter limit is read past; "except" or "save" there stays unread.
  - `parse_provision/1`: "This regulation applies in relation to England only".
- **`Readers.Application` (primary):**
  - a whole-instrument clause reaches every provision from its date, else from the start of the provision holding it;
  - a provision's own clause reaches it and those under it.
  An application narrows the territory and is never a version (extent is not application).
- **Golden data made honest:** SI 2012/3038 reg.1A now carries its CLML extent (E+W+S+NI), and NI comes from its own clause. Still 26 / 27; full suite 2,997 passing.
