## What good looks like (agreed with Jason 2026-10-22)

**Scope:** the OH&S core family (`💙 OH&S: Occupational / Personal Safety`, 149 LAT laws). The check takes any family.

**Needed, sliced by the gap the feed leaves** (revised 2026-10-22, Jason: "Are we slicing based on the defined gap after using the change feed" — "Yes, that's the right way"). A document is needed because a gap in the feed points to it, never only because it is one hop away. Gaps are read from the feed and the stored changes (`provision_changes`), so the check is re-run after each rebuild.

| # | need | for which laws | parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | own text | each law in scope | its LAT scope's documents |
| 2 | own feed | each law in scope | every page |
| 3 | territory (the feed is weak for every change) | every amending law of a law in scope | body (its extent and application clauses); for an SI stating no extent, its introduction (enabling powers) and those powers' parent Acts (body) |
| 4 | dates, gap A: the feed records the change but gives no date | the amending laws of those changes | its own commencement (body); an Act: its own feed and the orders it names commencing the provisions that made those changes |
| 5 | dates and the backlog, gap B: the feed doesn't record the change at all | the amending laws of those changes | body and schedules (the instruction itself); an Act: as in 4 |

**Pass when all hold:**
1. **The report**, `mix lat.sources --family F` (read-only, no fetch), lists needed against held by the five needs. Every missing item carries a reason: no XML on legislation.gov.uk, 404, fetch failed, or out of scope. Nothing missing without a reason.
2. **Stale enforced** (Jason: "Enforce stale now, we leave the session in a good place"): a held document older than legislation.gov.uk's latest (stored `dct:valid` before the law's register `md_dct_valid_date`; with no date, fetched over 30 days ago, `ClmlStore.fresh?`) fails the check until refetched or explained.
3. **The feed against the register's change statistics** (Jason: "Aren't there metrics in LRT for the change feed? Compare the counts"): per family law, our stored feed's effects and distinct affecting laws against `🔻_stats_affected_by_count` / `🔻_stats_affected_by_laws_count`. Equal, or the difference is explained (e.g. the register scraped earlier).
4. **Tested:** the counting rules above are unit-tested on hand-built data.
5. **Repeatable:** two runs give the same report.

## The check built (2026-10-22)

- **`Sources`** (pure): `needs/2` gives the seven categories; `report/3` gives counts, failures and register differences, and passes or fails; `register_counts/2` counts a feed as the register counts it.
- **`mix lat.sources --family F | --laws A,B [--all]`**: read-only, never fetches, about 1 min for the family.
- `ChangesFeed.page_path/4` and `EnablingCache.cached/1` (a lookup that never fetches) were added for it.
- **Row 7, made concrete:** an amending law's commencing orders are found only from its own feed. So row 7 covers amending Acts: their feed, and the orders it names. An SI commences itself in its own text.
- **A measure corrected before use.** The register's `🔻_stats_affected_by_count` counts effects by other laws that are not revocations (`Amending.build_stats_with_self`). Comparing all effects showed 75 false differences. Like for like there are 9.
